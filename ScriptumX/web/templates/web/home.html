Welcome to Scriptum𝓧

A Web-based Movie Script Editor and Production Planner (Alpha Version)

Scriptum𝓧 keeps a film project in one place: write and structure the script, break it into scenes and shots, then plan the shoot with roles, locations, and schedules. Reports and PDF export help you share call sheets and script pages with the crew.

Work in projects that hold one or more scripts. Edit scenes and dialogue in the browser, track roles and cast, and attach props, audio, and SFX where the breakdown needs them. Changes stay tied to the project so writers and production can use the same data.

Scenes link story locations and times to people and gear. From there you can move into shot lists, the planner (stripboard, day-out-of-days, cast conflicts), and appointments for shoot days.

Access is per project: producers invite writers, crew, and actors with role-based rights so each person sees the tabs they need—without exposing contact data or full backups to guests who only need the script.

List and matrix reports cover roles, persons, locations, gadgets, and scenes. Script read-out and PDF export are available when your membership allows script access. Share links can publish a filtered report without giving full login rights.

Log in to open your projects, or ask a producer or site admin for an account on this instance.
